# Sampling And Filtering Rules

## Recommended Search Order

1. `<剧目> repo`
2. `<剧目> 剧评`
3. `<剧目> 观后感`
4. `<剧目> 好看吗`
5. `<剧目> 剧情`
6. `<剧目> 音乐`
7. `<剧目> 舞美`
8. `<剧目> 避雷`
9. `<剧目> 差评`
10. `<剧目> 全场`
11. `<剧目> 盗摄`
12. `<剧目> 网盘`

For premiere reports, add date keywords if useful: `首演`, `中场repo`, `返场`, `首轮`.

## Include As Effective Repo

Include notes with at least one of:

- Specific story/character/pacing assessment.
- Music, melody, lyrics, orchestration, or vocal arrangement assessment.
- Staging, lighting, design, blocking, visual or theatre-space assessment.
- Acting/singing/performance assessment linked to the show experience.
- Clear overall recommendation with reasons.

## Exclude Or Downweight

Exclude from show-quality sample, but optionally count as heat/noise:

- Pure curtain call, encore, focus, cut, fancam, single-actor beauty/photo posts.
- Ticketing-only, seat-view-only, merch-only, fan support, poster reposts.
- Very short posts such as “太美了”, “好绝”, “冲”, with no show content.
- Posts clearly copied from official promo material.

## Infringement Risk Labels

- High: `全场`, `完整版`, `盗摄`, `录屏`, `网盘`, `下载`, `资源`, `私我发你`, actual link or request for full recording.
- Medium: long clips, bootleg-like wording, unclear “求视频/求音频”.
- Low: legal promo clips, official trailers, public curtain-call snippets unless venue rules prohibit them.

## Minimum Sample Discipline

For a professional report:

- Record total visible candidates.
- Open/read enough notes to cover multiple keywords, not only `repo`.
- Target at least 30 effective show-evaluation notes when available.
- If ranking by likes, still include lower-like negative/neutral notes from content-specific searches to avoid fan-ranking bias.
- State exclusions: for example, “excluded pure返场/focus/fan posts from show-evaluation sample”.

## Sentiment Tags

- 正向: clear recommendation or praise with reasons.
- 混合偏正: positive overall but with specific reservations.
- 中性/混合: balanced or descriptive without clear endorsement.
- 混合偏负: substantial criticism but not total rejection.
- 负向: clear avoid/踩雷/退票/难看 or multiple severe criticisms.

## Topic Tags

Use multiple tags when supported:

- 剧情/人物
- 音乐/歌词
- 舞美/视觉
- 演员/表演
- 导演/节奏
- 票务/场馆
- 传播热度
- 侵权风险

# Report Template

## Title

`《<剧目>》首演小红书 Repo 舆情报告`

## Executive Summary

Use 3-5 bullets:

- Overall sentiment and confidence level.
- Biggest positive driver.
- Biggest negative/controversial driver.
- Whether fan heat distorts apparent ranking.
- Immediate operational risk, especially infringement.

## Sample And Method

Include:

- Collection date and timezone.
- Monitoring window, such as `首演日起 48 小时内`.
- Search terms used.
- Sorting rule if known, such as likes-ranked visible notes.
- Sample counts:
  - Visible candidates.
  - Opened/read notes.
  - Effective show-evaluation repo notes.
  - Excluded fan/返场/focus notes.
  - Infringement-risk notes.

Recommended wording:

> 本报告基于小红书可见样本，不等同于全平台完整舆情。搜索排序受账号、时间、个性化推荐和平台风控影响。

## Sentiment Distribution

Table columns:

- 情绪
- 数量/占比
- 主要依据
- 代表主题

Keep fan/noise separate from effective show-evaluation sentiment.

## Topic Analysis

Use the following sections when evidence exists:

### 剧情与人物

Assess clarity, pacing, character motivation, emotional arc, adaptation logic, narrative density.

### 音乐与歌词

Assess melody memorability, lyric naturalness, theme recurrence, vocal difficulty, ensemble impact.

### 舞美与视觉

Assess scenic design, lighting, transitions, scale, theatre use, whether spectacle supports story.

### 演员与表演

Assess singing, acting, chemistry, line delivery, role fit. Avoid over-indexing on fandom unless it affects public reception.

### 传播与粉丝噪音

Separate:

- Heat from cast fandom.
- Repo content from pure返场/focus.
- Whether high-like posts are show-evaluation-rich or fan-media-heavy.

## Negative And Dispute Points

Group by cause:

- Text/lyrics
- Story/pacing
- Music memorability
- Production expectation vs ticket price
- Casting/performance debate
- Theatre/seat/technical issues

For each group include:

- What users object to.
- Severity.
- Whether it appears isolated or recurring.
- Suggested response or production adjustment.

## Infringement Risk

Include:

- Suspected post type: full show, bootleg clip, audio, cloud drive, resource request.
- Risk level.
- Evidence summary without amplifying links unnecessarily.
- Recommended action: screenshot evidence, preserve URL, report through platform, monitor repost chains.

## Representative Notes

Summarize, do not overquote:

| 类型 | 关键词/入口 | 互动 | 内容摘要 | 处理 |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|

Types:

- Positive effective repo
- Mixed effective repo
- Negative effective repo
- Fan/noise excluded
- Infringement risk

## Recommendations

Separate recommendations for:

- Production/creative team.
- Marketing/publicity.
- Fan operation.
- Legal/content protection.
- Follow-up monitoring.

## Final Caveats

State:

- Sample limitations.
- Ranking/personalization caveat.
- Whether additional scraping or manual review is needed before high-stakes decisions.
